Published anonymously in London on the first day of 1818, this novel was written by Mary Shelley when she was twenty. Its first edition carried a preface by her husband, Percy Bysshe Shelley, which led many early readers to guess that he was the author.

The story follows a young scientist who builds a living being and then abandons it. Many critics call it the first science fiction novel, although that label is debated.
